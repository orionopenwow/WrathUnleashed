        Add your game folder under CHANGE WOW FOLDER, pick a realm, press PLAY. Leyline writes the realm into realmlist.wtf (your original is kept as realmlist.wtf.leyline-backup) and starts the game. BROWSER plays Wrath in your web browser instead, with nothing to install.

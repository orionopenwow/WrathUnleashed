    Game Clients

    Point Leyline at a game folder you already have. It reads the build from the executable: 1.12.1 (5875), 2.4.3 (8606) or 3.3.5a (12340) for now. Any other build is listed as "other": you can play realms with it, but Leyline offers no mods for it. Leyline never downloads a game client.

    My Mods

    Drop a mod here a .zip, an addon folder, a client patch (.MPQ) or a native mod (.dll, .asi)

      Every mod is allowed: there is no review and no signing, so only add mods from people you trust. Workshop downloads must match their sha256, and anything holding game data or secrets is refused. Addons go to Interface\AddOns, patches to a free Data\patch-X.MPQ slot, native mods (.dll, .asi) next to the game, after a one-time OK. Leyline never runs what it installs. Removing a mod takes out exactly the files Leyline put in and you have not changed; an addon's saved settings in WTF stay, as the game keeps them.

      HOST YOUR OWN

      Community realms are the Leyline network. Anyone can run one from their own PC.

      1. Get the Host pack (on the Host page) and Docker.
      2. Pick a profile, a name and your house rules. The realm runs on your PC.
      3. Make it public: it shows up here for everyone, with no port forwarding. You are its GM.

      A private realm

      Got an invite link from a friend? Paste it here: Leyline opens the realm for this PC and points your client at it. Make your account on the link's page in your browser first (your password never leaves it).

      Another realm

      Host

      Run your own realm on this PC and let friends, or everyone, play on it. Your PC runs the server in Docker using your own 3.3.5a game files; it talks out to the Leyline relay, and players reach it through the relay. No port forwarding, and nobody sees your home address. We don't host realms for you: it runs only while your PC does.

      1. Make your realm on the website. Sign in, pick a name, a ruleset and your house rules, then download your realm file.
      2. Get the host kit and unzip it, then show Leyline where it is. You also need Docker Desktop.

        Playing

        Add your game folder under CHANGE GAME FOLDER, pick a realm, press PLAY. Leyline writes the realm into realmlist.wtf (your original is kept as realmlist.wtf.leyline-backup) and starts the game. BROWSER plays Wrath in your web browser instead, with nothing to install.

        Mods

        Discover lists the Workshop. INSTALL puts a mod into the client you picked; My Mods turns it off and on, updates it and removes it. You can also drop a .zip, an addon folder or a .MPQ patch on My Mods.

        Realms and hosting

        Realms lists the official realm and the community's Beacons. PLAY sets your realmlist and starts the game; BACK TO WRATH points it at the official realm again. Host walks you through running your own.

        Updates

        Leyline looks for a new version when it starts and every 6 hours. It downloads it in the background, checks its signature, and installs it only when you press Update ready, restart in the title bar, never while a game you started from Leyline is open.

        Privacy

        No telemetry and no accounts in the launcher. It fetches the Workshop catalog and the mods you install, the mod rankings, the realm list and realm status, and GitHub's release list and Leyline's own updates; it keeps copies of the lists so it works offline, and opens only pages it listed itself in your browser. Each request gives up after 8 seconds.

        Find your game files

        Leyline plays with a game folder you already have. Leyline can't download the game for you.

        LOOK IN THE USUAL PLACES
        • Windows C:\Program Files (x86), C:\Games, your Desktop or Downloads
        • Mac Applications or your home folder
        • Linux ~/Games, or a Wine, Lutris or Bottles prefix under drive_c

        The right folder holds the game's program and a Data folder.

        RESTORE A COPY

        An old drive or PC, a backup, or a cloud copy: put the whole folder back on this PC, then choose it.

        ASK A FRIEND

        A friend with the same version can copy their game folder to you, on a USB drive for example.

        NO FILES?

        Play in your browser: Wrath realms stream there, with nothing to install.

        Help

        Playing

        Add your game folder, pick a realm, press PLAY. Leyline sets the realmlist (your original is kept as a backup). BROWSER plays in your web browser instead.

        Mods

        Mods › Discover lists the Workshop; INSTALL adds a mod to your picked client. Mods › Installed turns mods off, on, updates and removes them, or takes a dropped .zip, folder or .MPQ.

        Realms and hosting

        Realms lists the official realm and community Beacons; PLAY joins one. MODES lists minigames. Host runs your own.

        Community

        Community › Friends: friends and messages. Community › Hub: Discord, Workshop and source.
